SPEC-DB-01 • Schema Model
User & Identity
• User: id, nik, email, password, role, isVerified, createdAt
• Profile: id, userId, fullName, phone, address, kabKota, dapil
• Dewan: id, userId, nama, fraksi, komisi, dapilId, fotoUrl
Aspirasi & E-Audiensi
• Aspirasi: id, ticketNo, userId, title, description, categoryId, status, komisi
• Schedule: id, dewanId, aspirasiId, scheduledAt, status, roomName
• RoomSession: id, scheduleId, roomSid, duration, recordingUrl, transcript
Disposisi OPD & Audit
• FollowUp: id, aspirasiId, opdId, suratNo, step, status, proofUrl, notes
• OPD: id, name, code, sector, contactPerson, email, phone
• AuditLog: id, userId, action, ipAddress, userAgent, details, timestamp
Integritas Relasional: Menjamin kepatuhan prinsip ACID, kunci asing (Foreign Keys) berindeks, dan audit jejak transaksi perbankan/pemerintahan.
